Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Who made this app

YoutubeAutomation is a private tool built and owned by Jaseem Feroz. It is used to publish short videos to one YouTube channel only. It is not a product, is not for sale, and collects no information from visitors to this page.

What the app can do

The app can do exactly one thing:

Upload videos to a YouTube channel. That is the only permission it asks for. It cannot read your channel, change its settings, delete videos, or access any other part of your Google account.

What it stores

  • Nothing is collected about you, and no analytics or tracking is used.
  • The app keeps a short internal list of which videos it has already published, so it does not post the same one twice.
  • That list stays on the machine running the app. It is never sold, shared, or sent anywhere else.

What it does not do

  • It does not read or store your Google or YouTube password.
  • It does not access your email, contacts, files, or other Google services.
  • It does not show advertising, and it is not used to track anyone.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google account connections page. Removing YoutubeAutomation there stops all access immediately and permanently.
